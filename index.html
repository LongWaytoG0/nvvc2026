Scoring

Track 1 · Understanding

Objective automatic evaluation only. The per-language score is:

$$\text{Track1Score}=100\times\bigl[\,0.70\,F1_{\text{micro}}+0.20\,(1-\text{mNTD})+0.10\,(1-\min(\text{Err}_{\text{tagged}},1))\,\bigr]$$
  • Event-based Micro F1 (0.70) — pools NVV events across categories; an event is correct only with the right category and transcript-relative position.
  • mNTD (0.20) — placement error between predicted and reference NVV tags; lower is better.
  • Tagged-transcript Error (0.10) — tag-aware CER (Mandarin) / WER (English), with each NVV tag treated as one atomic token.

Bilingual score is the mean of the two languages:

$$\text{FinalTrack1Score}=\tfrac{1}{2}\left(\text{Track1Score}_{\text{ZH}}+\text{Track1Score}_{\text{EN}}\right)$$

Track 2 · Generation

Each system is rated on five dimensions per language:

$$\text{Track2Score}=100\times\left(0.30A+0.25P+0.15N+0.15Q+0.15E\right)$$
  • A · NVV Accuracy (0.30) — whether the intended NVV types, numbers, and positions are correctly realized (0 if absent / inaudible).
  • P · NVV Perceptual Effect (0.25) — whether NVVs are clearly audible, recognizable, and perceptually effective.
  • N · Overall Naturalness (0.15) — naturalness, fluency, and acoustic continuity of the full utterance.
  • Q · Overall Quality (0.15) — perceived audio quality, including the absence of noticeable artifacts.
  • E · Overall Expression (0.15) — expressive delivery coherent and appropriate for the content, target NVVs, and conversational context.

Bilingual score is the mean of the two languages:

$$\text{FinalTrack2Score}=\tfrac{1}{2}\left(\text{Track2Score}_{\text{ZH}}+\text{Track2Score}_{\text{EN}}\right)$$

The five dimensions are scored two ways — automatically by a large audio-language model (LALM), the Objective score, and by a human listening test, the Subjective score. The overall ranking combines them:

$$\text{FinalScore}=0.30\times\text{Objective}+0.70\times\text{Subjective}$$

Tie-break: higher NVV Accuracy, then NVV Perceptual Effect, then Overall Naturalness.
