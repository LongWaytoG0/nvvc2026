Scoring

  • Track 1 (Understanding) — per-language scores derive from detection and transcription metrics (ZH from F1 / mNTD / CER; EN from F1 / mNTD / WER); the reported score is the ZH–EN mean. Objective automatic evaluation only.
  • Track 2 rubric — every system is rated on the same five dimensions, normalized to 0–100: $$\text{Track2Score}=\operatorname{norm}\!\left(0.30\,A+0.25\,P+0.15\,N+0.15\,Q+0.15\,E\right)$$ A = NVV Accuracy, P = NVV Perceptual Effect, N = Overall Naturalness, Q = Overall Quality, E = Overall Expression. Bilingual is the ZH–EN mean.
  • Objective — the five dimensions rated automatically by a large audio-language model (LALM). Subjective — the same five dimensions from a human listening test (v2).
  • Track 2 overall — the final ranking blends both: $$\text{FinalScore}=0.30\times\text{Objective}+0.70\times\text{Subjective}$$
